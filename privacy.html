Privacy Policy

Updated 17 November 2026

We store your Google account identifier, optional profile details, study progress, saved items, content reports and activity needed to provide revision tools.

Google sign-in and cloud data use Supabase.

Optional details can be omitted.

We do not use advertising trackers, collect raw IP addresses in our application analytics, or request precise location.

Functional browser storage keeps your session, preferences and unsynced progress.

Downloaded offline content stays on this browser until sign-out or removal.

You can export your study data in Settings and request account deletion there once the database is connected.

Account-linked analytics are removed or anonymised on deletion.

Service providers may maintain their own security logs.

Content reports are retained to resolve errors.

For a privacy request, use the in-app feedback tool; contact details will be added when the owner configures the production service.
